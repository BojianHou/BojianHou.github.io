Bojian Hou

also published as Bo-Jian Hou · 侯博建

I am an AI Research Scientist at Meta in Monetization and Meta Recommendation Systems (MRS). My work centers on LLM-style model architectures and scaling laws for massive-scale ads ranking (e.g., Kunlun, KDD 2026) and on representation learning that connects organic and ads signals for ranking and retrieval. I also continue my research on trustworthy large language models, especially calibration, uncertainty estimation, and preference alignment.

Before joining Meta, I was a postdoctoral researcher in the Department of Biostatistics, Epidemiology and Informatics at the University of Pennsylvania, advised by Prof. Li Shen, where I led a Trustworthy AI research line and mentored 20+ students. Earlier, I was a postdoctoral associate at Cornell University. I received my B.Sc. and Ph.D. in Computer Science from Nanjing University in 2014 and 2020, supervised by Prof. Zhi-Hua Zhou in the LAMDA Group.

I am interested in both the theoretical foundations and the real-world deployment of machine learning, and in building AI systems that are technically rigorous, trustworthy, and socially responsible.
